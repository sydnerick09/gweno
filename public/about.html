About Gweno

Who we are and why this website exists.

Our mission

Gweno is a task platform. People post the work they need help with, and a community of doers picks it up and gets it done. We built Gweno to make that exchange as simple, fair and safe as possible — no bidding wars, no red tape.

Why people choose us

  • Simple: post a task in plain language, or browse tasks you can do.
  • Secure: hashed passwords, expiring sessions, and single-use reset links.
  • Rewarding: new members earn a small KES welcome bonus for joining.
  • Human: real ratings and easy communication keep everyone accountable.

How it works

Someone posts a task and what it's worth. Someone else takes it on, does the work, and submits it. Once it's approved, the doer gets paid and both sides leave a rating. That's it.

Where we're going

We're just getting started. Payments, richer task categories and a mobile app are all on the way — built around the same idea: tasks in, results out.
