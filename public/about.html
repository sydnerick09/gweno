About Gweno

Who we are and why this website exists.

Our mission

Gweno is an online work-from-home platform. We help people around the world earn from home by completing simple online tasks and surveys — on their own schedule, from any device. Our goal is to make earning online simple, fair and safe for everyone.

Where we're based

Gweno is based in the United States and operates entirely online, so members can work and get paid from anywhere in the world. There's nothing to commute to — your work happens wherever you are.

Why people choose us

  • Work from home: earn online in your spare time, from any device.
  • Worldwide: join from anywhere and withdraw to M-Pesa, PayPal or your bank.
  • Secure: hashed passwords, expiring sessions, and single-use reset links.
  • Rewarding: new members earn a small welcome bonus just for joining.

How it works

Pick a task or survey, follow the instructions, and submit your proof. Once it's approved, your reward is added to your balance — ready to cash out to your preferred payment method. Simple.

Where we're going

We're just getting started. More payment options, richer task categories and a mobile app are all on the way — built around the same idea: work from home, earn online, get paid.
