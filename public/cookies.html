Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026 · This is a plain-language summary for a demo.

What cookies we use

Gweno keeps things minimal. We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in:

  • gweno_session — an HTTP-only cookie that remembers your sign-in. It's readable only by the server (page scripts can't touch it) and it expires on its own, so a session can never live forever.
  • gweno_admin — the same idea, but only ever set on the separate admin panel.

What we don't do

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, and no selling of your browsing data. We don't follow you around the web.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Note that if you block the session cookie, you won't be able to stay signed in.

Contact

Questions about cookies? Email privacy@gweno.app.
