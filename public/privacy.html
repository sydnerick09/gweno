Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Gweno collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Gweno you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details, your name, email, username, and password (stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile details, country, phone number, and anything you add in your profile or the welcome questionnaire.
  • Payment details, the M-Pesa phone number, PayPal email, or bank details you enter for deposits and withdrawals. We do not store full card numbers, card payments are handled on the payment provider's secure page.
  • Activity, tasks and surveys you complete, deposits, withdrawals, investments, and referrals.
  • Technical data, sign-in sessions and basic device/usage information needed to keep your account secure.

2. How we use it

To operate your account and wallet, process deposits, withdrawals and investments, credit your earnings, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, and improve the Platform.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with the payment providers that process your transactions, M-Pesa (Safaricom Daraja), Paystack, and PayPal, and with our hosting and database provider. We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Cookies & sessions

We use a secure, http-only session cookie to keep you signed in. Sessions expire on their own and resetting your password signs you out everywhere. See our Cookie Policy for detail.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions have a limited lifetime and are checked on every request, password-reset links are single-use and expire, and repeated failed logins are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your password and never share it.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is open and as needed for legal, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. You can delete your account in Settings; some records may be retained where the law requires.

7. Your rights

You can access and update your profile in Settings, request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it. Contact us via Help & support.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; we'll flag material changes on the site. Continued use means you accept the current version.
