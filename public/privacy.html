Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · This is a plain-language summary for a demo.

What we collect

Your name and email, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text), and the welcome answers you choose to share — your age, education, a short bio, and how you heard about us.

How we use it

To run your account, keep it secure, credit your welcome bonus, and improve the platform. We do not sell your personal data.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
  • Sign-in sessions expire automatically and can't live forever.
  • Password-reset links are single-use and time-limited.
  • Session cookies are HTTP-only so page scripts can't read them.

Your choices

The welcome questions are optional — answer only what you're comfortable with. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@gweno.app.
