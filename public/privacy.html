Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Gweno ("Gweno", "we", "us", "our", or the "Platform") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, applications, services, wallet features, tasks, surveys, subscription plans, deposits, withdrawals, referrals, and other services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions. By creating an account or using Gweno, you acknowledge that you have read and understood both the Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions. Where you use payment, subscription, withdrawal, identity verification, or other financial features, additional information may be processed as necessary to operate and secure those services.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not create an account or use the Platform.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to create and operate your account, provide Platform services, process transactions, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password

Passwords are protected using secure hashing and are not stored in plain text.

As explained in our Terms & Conditions, users are responsible for providing accurate, current, and truthful information. Where identity verification is required, information provided to Gweno may need to match your official identification credentials, such as a National ID, passport, or another accepted government-issued document.

Profile and verification information

We may collect:

  • Country or region
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth where required
  • Information entered into your profile
  • Information provided through welcome questionnaires
  • Identity verification information or documents where required
  • Information required to confirm account ownership or prevent fraud

You should not provide another person's personal information unless you are legally authorized to do so.

2. Payment and financial information

Gweno may collect payment and payout information necessary to process deposits, subscriptions, withdrawals, and other supported transactions. This may include:

  • M-Pesa phone number
  • PayPal email address
  • Bank account details
  • Payment references or transaction IDs
  • Payment status and transaction history
  • Subscription and plan information
  • Withdrawal details

Card payments may be processed directly through the secure systems of the applicable payment provider. Gweno does not intentionally store full card numbers or complete card security credentials.

We may process payment-related information to confirm whether a transaction was successful, prevent duplicate transactions, activate the correct subscription plan, process withdrawals, investigate failed payments, and maintain financial and audit records. A payment provider may process your information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

3. Activity and Platform usage information

When you use Gweno, we may collect information about your activity, including:

  • Tasks and surveys you access or complete
  • Submission and approval history
  • Earnings and wallet activity
  • Deposits and withdrawals
  • Subscription plan activity
  • Referral activity
  • Support requests and communications
  • Account status and security events

This information helps us operate the Platform, review completed work, process earnings, identify errors, enforce task and subscription rules, and protect against fraud or abuse.

4. Technical and security information

We may automatically collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the Platform. This may include:

  • Sign-in sessions
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address or approximate location information where required for security
  • Login timestamps
  • Security logs
  • Error reports
  • Basic usage information

We use this information to protect accounts, detect suspicious activity, troubleshoot technical problems, prevent unauthorized access, and improve the reliability of the Platform. We do not use technical information to intentionally collect unnecessary personal data.

5. How we use your information

Gweno may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity where necessary
  • Display and update your profile
  • Operate your wallet and account balance
  • Process deposits and subscription payments
  • Activate or update your subscription plan after verified payment
  • Process and review withdrawal requests
  • Credit approved task and survey earnings
  • Review submissions for quality and compliance
  • Prevent fraud, duplicate accounts, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate important account, payment, and security information
  • Maintain records required for accounting, auditing, or legal purposes
  • Improve Platform performance and security

Your information may also be used to enforce the Terms & Conditions. For example, account information, task activity, payment records, and identity details may be reviewed where there is a reasonable need to investigate fraud, duplicate accounts, false information, or other violations of the Platform rules.

6. How this policy connects with the Terms & Conditions

The Terms & Conditions establish the rules for using Gweno, while this Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used to operate those rules and services.

For example, our Terms require users to provide correct information. This Privacy Policy explains that we may process that information to verify accounts and reduce payment errors or fraud. Our Terms also explain task reviews, subscriptions, deposits, withdrawals, account restrictions, and fraud prevention. To operate these features, Gweno may process relevant account, activity, transaction, and security information.

Providing inaccurate or misleading information may affect our ability to verify your account or process transactions. Where necessary and permitted by applicable law, Gweno may request additional information before processing a withdrawal, activating certain services, or resolving an account issue.

7. Who we share information with

Gweno does not sell your personal data. We may share information only where reasonably necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations. This may include sharing relevant information with:

  • Payment providers and processors
  • M-Pesa and Safaricom Daraja-supported payment services
  • Paystack
  • PayPal
  • Banks or other supported financial institutions
  • Hosting, infrastructure, database, and security providers
  • Professional advisers where legally necessary
  • Government authorities or law-enforcement agencies where required by law

We share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. For example, a payment provider may need your transaction details to confirm a payment, while a payout provider may require your name and payment information to process a withdrawal. Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies governing the information they process.

8. Cookies and sessions

Gweno may use cookies and secure session technologies to keep you signed in and protect your account. Where supported by the Platform, session cookies may be configured to reduce unauthorized access and may expire automatically.

We may also use technical storage necessary for security, authentication, preferences, and essential Platform functionality. Resetting your password or taking certain security actions may invalidate existing sessions and require you to sign in again.

You can manage certain cookie settings through your browser, and change your advertising-cookie choice on our Cookie Policy page. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the Platform.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. Security measures may include:

  • Password hashing
  • Limited session lifetimes
  • Session validation
  • Expiring and single-use password-reset links
  • Rate limiting for repeated failed login attempts
  • Access controls
  • Security monitoring
  • Transaction verification
  • Fraud and abuse detection

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and should never share your login credentials or security codes with another person. If you believe your account has been accessed without authorization, contact Gweno through the official Help & Support section as soon as possible.

10. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate your account and provide our services. Some information may be retained after account closure where necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Legal compliance
  • Accounting and financial records
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Dispute resolution
  • Enforcement of our Terms & Conditions

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

11. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Update certain profile details
  • Request a copy of information associated with your account
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Ask questions about how your information is processed

You can update certain information through your account settings. Where identity or transaction information is required for security, legal, or financial purposes, we may be unable to immediately delete or modify certain records. To make a privacy-related request, contact us through the official Help & Support section.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Gweno may update this Privacy Policy as the Platform develops, legal requirements change, or our data practices are updated. Where appropriate, material changes may be communicated through the Platform, email, notifications, or another reasonable method. The updated version will display a revised "Last Updated" date.

Your continued use of Gweno after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the revised policy, subject to any rights available to you under applicable law.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, account security, or your privacy rights, please contact Gweno through the official Help & Support section of the Platform.

You should also review this Privacy Policy together with the Terms & Conditions, as both documents apply to your use of Gweno.

By creating an account or continuing to use Gweno, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the processing of your information as described, subject to applicable law.
