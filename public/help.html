Help Center

Answers to the questions we hear most. Still need help? Contact us through Help & Support.

The information below should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Community Rules. These documents explain the requirements for using Gweno, protecting your account, completing tasks, and processing payments.

Getting started

How do I create an account?

Tap Get Started and register using your email address and a password. After registration, you may be asked to complete a short welcome questionnaire and provide basic profile information.

Please ensure that the information you provide is correct and up to date. Where account or payment verification is required, your details may need to match your official identification and payment credentials. You are responsible for protecting your account and should never share your password or security codes with another person.

Is Gweno free to join?

Yes. Creating a Gweno account is free. Some optional services, subscription plans, upgrades, deposits, or other Platform features may have fees, which will be shown before you confirm the relevant transaction.

Eligible users may also receive a welcome reward or access to introductory opportunities after completing required profile or welcome information, subject to the current Platform rules.

Earning

How do I earn on Gweno?

You can earn by completing eligible tasks and surveys available through the Platform. Gweno may also provide referral opportunities and other approved earning activities. Task availability and reward values may depend on your subscription plan, account eligibility, and current Platform rules. Rewards are added to your wallet only after the relevant task or survey has been successfully completed and approved.

Why was my submission rejected?

Submissions must follow the instructions provided for each task. Work may be rejected if it is inaccurate, incomplete, duplicated, copied, misleading, or otherwise does not meet the required standards. Submitting work does not automatically guarantee payment. Please complete tasks honestly and carefully before submitting them for review.

Deposits

How do I add money or make a payment?

Go to Redeem → Deposits and choose an available payment method. Depending on your location and the available services, options may include:

  • M-Pesa: enter the amount in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and approve the payment request on your phone.
  • Card or supported payment provider: you may be redirected to a secure payment page to complete the transaction.
  • Bank transfer: follow the account details and payment instructions displayed on the Platform.

Your account or subscription is updated after the payment provider confirms the transaction and Gweno successfully verifies it.

Investments

How do investments work?

If investment features are available, you may select an eligible investment plan, choose an amount, review the applicable terms, and complete payment using an available method. An investment becomes active only after payment is successfully confirmed. Any stated rate, lock period, maturity date, or other conditions will be displayed before you confirm.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment activities may involve risk, and returns are not guaranteed unless a specific guarantee is legally valid and clearly stated. Only participate with funds you can afford to commit or potentially lose. Please review the applicable Terms & Conditions before making an investment.

Withdrawals and payouts

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Go to Redeem → Payouts and select your preferred available withdrawal method. Depending on the service available to your account, you may be able to use:

  • M-Pesa, with payouts processed in KES
  • PayPal, where supported
  • Bank transfer, where supported

Enter the requested amount and carefully confirm your payout details before submitting. Your name and payment information should be accurate and, where verification is required, match your legitimate account and identification credentials.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Withdrawal processing times may vary depending on verification requirements, payment-provider availability, and account status. Requests may be reviewed before funds are sent. Where a withdrawal is rejected or cannot be completed, the treatment of the funds will follow the applicable Platform rules and transaction status.

Account and security

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Select Forgot Password on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. Password-reset links are intended to be secure, may be single-use, and can expire after a limited period.

How does Gweno protect my account?

Gweno uses security measures designed to protect accounts and reduce unauthorized access, including password hashing, session controls, security checks, and other protective measures. However, you also have an important role in keeping your account secure:

  • Never share your password.
  • Never share verification or security codes.
  • Use accurate account information.
  • Sign out of shared devices.
  • Report suspicious activity through Help & Support.

Gweno staff should not ask you to reveal your password. If someone requests your password while claiming to represent Gweno, do not provide it.

Still need help?

If your question is not answered here, contact us through the official Help & Support section of the Platform. When contacting support, provide accurate information about your account or transaction so that your request can be reviewed efficiently.

For payment, withdrawal, account verification, or security issues, please include only the information requested through official Gweno support channels and avoid sharing your password or other sensitive security credentials.
