Help Center

Answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Contact support.

Getting started

How do I create an account? Tap Get started and sign up with your email, or with Google, Facebook or Apple. Answer a few quick welcome questions and you're in.

Is Gweno free to join? Yes. Creating an account is free, and you earn a small welcome bonus for completing your profile.

Earning

How do I earn? Complete tasks and surveys from the Earn section, and refer friends for a bonus. Approved rewards are added to your wallet.

Why was my submission rejected? Submissions need accurate proof and must follow the task instructions. Fake or duplicated work can't be paid.

Deposits

How do I top up my wallet? Go to Redeem → Deposit and choose a method:

  • M-Pesa, enter the amount in KES and approve the STK prompt on your phone.
  • Card / Paystack, you're taken to a secure card page to pay. Your wallet is credited automatically once the payment succeeds.
  • Bank transfer, you'll see the account to send to; your wallet is credited once we confirm the transfer.

Investments

How do investments work? Pick a plan (Starter, Growth or Premium), choose an amount, and pay with your preferred method. The investment activates once payment is confirmed and pays a fixed rate of interest over the lock period. At maturity, principal plus interest is credited to your wallet.

Are returns guaranteed? Stated rates are targets, not guarantees. Investing carries risk, so only invest what you can afford to lose. See our Terms.

Withdrawals

How do I cash out? Go to Redeem → Withdraw, choose M-Pesa (paid in KES), PayPal or bank (paid in USD), enter the amount and your details, and submit.

How long does it take? Withdrawals are usually verified by our team within 2 hours before funds are sent. The amount is held from your balance when you request it and refunded if the withdrawal is rejected.

Account & security

I forgot my password. Use Forgot password to get a single-use reset link.

How do you keep my account safe? Passwords are hashed, sessions expire automatically, and reset links are single-use. Never share your password, we'll never ask for it.
