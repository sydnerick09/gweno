Help & Support

We're here to help — most messages are answered within 24 hours.

Before you contact us

Many questions are answered in our Help Center — how to deposit, invest, withdraw, and keep your account secure.

Ways to reach us

  • In-app: the fastest way — sign in and open Support from the menu to send us a message tied to your account.
  • Email: support@gweno.app

When contacting us, please include

  • The email on your account
  • What you were trying to do (deposit, withdraw, invest, a task, etc.)
  • Any reference or transaction ID, and a screenshot if you have one

Please never share your password — our team will never ask for it.

Response times

Support messages: within 24 hours on business days. Withdrawals are verified and sent usually within 24 hours. Task submissions are typically reviewed within a few hours.
