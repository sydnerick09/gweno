Community Rules

Last updated: July 2026

Gweno works because members treat each other and the platform fairly. These rules keep Gweno safe and rewarding for everyone. Breaking them can lead to rejected work, a suspended account, or forfeited earnings.

1. Be honest

  • Only submit real, genuine work. Fake proof, bots, auto-clickers or copied answers are not allowed.
  • Complete each task as instructed, don't cut corners or claim work you didn't do.
  • Give truthful answers in surveys and in your welcome questionnaire.

2. One person, one account

You may hold one account per person and per device. Creating multiple accounts to farm bonuses, referrals or tasks is fraud and will result in suspension and loss of earnings. Your username is unique, once it's claimed, no one else can use it.

3. Play fair with referrals

Refer real people who genuinely join and complete the welcome questions. Self-referrals, fake sign-ups and spammy sharing are not allowed.

4. Keep it respectful & legal

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or abusive behaviour.
  • No illegal activity, money laundering, or attempts to defraud Gweno or its members.
  • No explicit, adult or offensive profile pictures or content.

5. Protect your account

Never share your password, and never share it if someone asks, Gweno staff will never ask for it. You are responsible for activity on your account.

6. Payments & withdrawals

Use your own payment details for deposits and withdrawals. Every withdrawal is reviewed and verified before it is sent. Attempting to abuse the payment system will lead to a permanent ban.

7. Reporting

If you see abuse, fraud or anything that breaks these rules, tell us through Help & support. We review every report.
